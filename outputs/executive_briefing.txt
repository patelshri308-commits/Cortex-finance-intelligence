## Headline
The company's Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) decreased by 1.16% to $40,384,499, a decline of $475,882 from the previous month.

## Performance Overview
- New business (Bookings) increased by $11,175 (0.09%) to $12,899,531.
- Expansion Revenue grew by $295,460 (6.01%) from the previous month.
- Churned Revenue decreased by $47,946 (7.78%) from the previous month.
- Contraction Revenue increased by $181,490 from the previous month.
- Renewal Revenue declined by $255,435, contributing to the net decline in ARR.
- The net effect on ARR was a decrease of $475,882, driven primarily by the decline in Renewal Revenue.

## Key Risk
The structural challenge revealed by the data is the decline in Renewal Revenue, which decreased by $255,435, indicating potential issues with customer retention.

## Metrics To Monitor
- Renewal Revenue: to understand the drivers behind the decline and identify opportunities for improvement.
- Contraction Revenue: to determine if the increase is a one-time event or an emerging trend.
- Expansion Revenue: to identify areas for further investment and potential upside opportunities.
- Churned Revenue: to monitor the trend and ensure the decline is sustained.

## Leadership Takeaway
The decline in Renewal Revenue, which accounts for the majority of the ARR decrease, highlights the need for the company to focus on customer retention and identify opportunities to improve renewal rates.
