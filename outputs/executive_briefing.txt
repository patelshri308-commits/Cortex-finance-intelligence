## Headline
The company's Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) decreased by 1.16% to $40,384,499, a decline of $475,882 from the previous month.

## Performance Overview
- New business revenue increased by $212,261.
- Expansion revenue increased by $295,460, a 5.99% growth.
- Renewal revenue decreased by $255,435.
- Contraction revenue increased by $181,490.
- Churned revenue decreased by $47,946.
- The net effect on ARR was a decline of $475,882, driven by the decrease in renewal revenue and increase in contraction revenue, which outweighed the positive impact of expansion revenue growth.

## Key Risk
The structural challenge revealed by the data is the decline in renewal revenue, which was the primary driver of the ARR decrease, highlighting a potential issue with customer retention.

## Metrics To Monitor
- Renewal revenue: closely monitor the trend and investigate the root causes of the decline.
- Contraction revenue: track the increase and determine if there are opportunities to mitigate this trend.
- Expansion revenue: analyze the growth drivers to understand the potential for future growth.
- Churned revenue: continue to monitor the trend and investigate the impact on ARR.

## Leadership Takeaway
The primary challenge facing the company is the decline in renewal revenue, which highlights the need to focus on improving customer retention and renewal rates to stabilize and grow ARR.
