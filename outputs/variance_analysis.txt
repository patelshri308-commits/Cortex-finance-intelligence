### Variance Summary
* **ARR Contraction:** December ARR declined by **-$475.9k (-1.16% MoM)** to **$40.38M**, reversing the growth momentum from November ($40.86M).
* **Flat Bookings Velocity:** Monthly bookings remained essentially flat MoM at **$12.90M (+$11.2k / +0.09%)**, indicating that steady sales transactional volume failed to translate into net-positive ARR.
* **Leaky Bucket Dynamics:** Despite strong customer acquisition and expansion performance, ARR was heavily dragged down by severe headwinds in renewal execution and elevated contraction.

### Primary Drivers
* **Strong Expansion & New Business Tailwinds:** Expansion revenue increased by **+$295.5k MoM** and New Business grew by **+$212.3k MoM**, signaling healthy demand, strong cross-sell/up-sell motions, and solid net-new customer acquisition.
* **Severe Renewal & Contraction Compression:** These gains were entirely wiped out by a **-$255.4k decline in renewal revenue** and a **+$181.5k increase in contraction revenue (down-sells)**, indicating aggressive customer-side optimization and down-sizing during December contract renewals.
* **Elevated Year-End Churn:** Churned revenue worsened by **-$47.9k MoM**, compounding the renewal pressure and highlighting critical customer success or platform-adoption friction at the close of the fiscal year.

### Risks / Watchouts
* **Renewals & Down-Sell Exposure:** The simultaneous drop in renewal revenue (-$255.4k) and spike in contraction (+$181.5k) suggest a systemic trend of customers renegotiating smaller footprints or consolidating licenses at renewal. If this trend carries into Q1, it poses a material threat to net retention rate (NRR) targets.
* **Bookings-to-ARR Conversion Lag:** While bookings held steady at ~$12.90M, ARR contracted. This divergence points to potential implementation bottlenecks, delayed go-live schedules, or deferred contract start dates that are pushing ARR recognition out into future quarters.

### Recommended Follow-Up
* **Execute a Renewal & Contraction Post-Mortem:** Task the Customer Success and Account Management heads to audit the December renewal cohort. Identify the specific accounts behind the $255.4k renewal decline and the $181.5k contraction to determine if this was driven by aggressive pricing discounts, seat reductions, or product performance issues.
* **Audit the Provisioning Pipeline:** Direct the Operations and Professional Services teams to review all pending bookings from Q4. Identify and remove blockers preventing booked contracts from being provisioned, accelerating the "bookings-to-ARR" conversion timeline to bolster early Q1 ARR.
